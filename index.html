Explore the essential components of a gaming PC and learn how to build your own computer.

Explore PC Components

Processor (CPU)

The processor handles instructions and performs calculations for your computer.

Details
Consider core count, clock speed and motherboard compatibility when choosing a CPU.

Choosing a CPU

AMD Ryzen and Intel Core processors offer different performance options for gaming and productivity.

Graphics Card (GPU)

The graphics card renders images and delivers smooth gaming performance.

Details
Graphics cards differ in performance, video memory and power consumption.

Choosing a GPU

Choose a graphics card based on your monitor resolution, desired frame rate and budget.

Motherboard

The motherboard connects all the major components of your computer.

Details
Motherboards come in different sizes and support specific processor sockets and memory types.

Choosing a Motherboard

Make sure your motherboard supports your processor, RAM, storage devices and case size.

Memory (RAM)

RAM temporarily stores data that your computer is actively using.

Details
Memory capacity and speed can affect multitasking and gaming performance.

Choosing RAM

Between 16GB and 32GB of RAM is a practical choice for most modern gaming computers.

Storage (SSD)

Storage devices hold your operating system, games and personal files.

Details
SSDs provide faster loading times than traditional mechanical hard drives.

Choosing Storage

A 1TB NVMe SSD provides a useful starting point for storing games and everyday applications.

Power Supply (PSU)

The power supply delivers electricity to the internal components of your PC.

Details
Power supplies vary in wattage, efficiency ratings and cable configurations.

Choosing a Power Supply

Select a reliable power supply with enough wattage to support all your components.
